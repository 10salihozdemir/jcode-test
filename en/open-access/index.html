OPEN ACCESS POLICY

JCoDe: Journal of Computational Design is an open-access publication.

The journal’s content is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC 4.0). This licensing framework ensures that third parties can share, adapt, and utilize the material for non-commercial purposes, as long as appropriate acknowledgment is given to the original source. By enabling these permissions, the journal supports the widespread sharing and application of its published research while upholding non-commercial usage guidelines.
