For Authors

Submission steps, author guidelines and templates.

Submission steps

  1. Prepare

    Read the author guidelines. Prepare the manuscript with the Word template and add the cover page as a separate file.

  2. Submit

    Upload the manuscript with your DergiPark account. Add the copyright and author agreement and the similarity report.

  3. Follow the process

    The initial check and peer review run on DergiPark. Correspondence and revisions are handled there.

  4. Publication

    Accepted manuscripts are typeset, assigned a DOI and published with the issue.
